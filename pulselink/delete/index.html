Delete my data

Everything PulseLink stores for you is attached to your watch's Device ID. Enter that ID below and all of it is erased from our server.

What gets erased

  • Your nickname and the record that this Device ID ever used PulseLink.
  • Every image you uploaded — all five watch face slots and every stored camera frame.
  • Your Crew membership, your messages, and your join requests.
  • If you are a Crew Leader, the Crew itself is deleted and every Member is removed from it.

This cannot be undone. Your license or trial is held separately and is not affected — open the app again and you can start over with the same Device ID.

Confirm your Device ID

On the watch: You menu > Link up > Device ID.
