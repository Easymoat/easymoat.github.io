How it works

Two pieces: The Face on the watch, Face Composer on the web

The Face is a Connect IQ watch face that shows whatever you compose. It checks for a new dial every 5 minutes, so you never plug anything in.

1

Install The Face

From the Connect IQ Store. The watch face shows your Device ID (XXXX-XXXX) at the bottom of the dial.

2

Open Face Composer

On a computer, type the Device ID once. That is the whole sign-in: there is no account and no password.

3

Compose

Drag parts onto the dial, pick a photo and frame it, choose colours and sizes. Save it to your Collection (up to 30).

4

Send

Press To watch. Within 5 minutes the watch shows it. Switch between saved dials from your phone's browser, anywhere.

Features

Everything on the dial is a part you place

🧩

Free placement

Time, date, battery, seconds, notifications, hands, rim ticks and meters, lines, circles and rectangles. Put them anywhere, with undo, align and multi-select.

🖼️

Your photo, framed your way

Square, circle, heart, star and more. Zoom, move, brightness and saturation. The cut-out travels to the watch in 32 colours, tuned for AMOLED.

❤️

Body and weather

Heart rate, steps, floors, calories, weight, Body Battery, stress, respiration. Weather now, humidity, wind, pressure, altitude, rain, moon age, today's high/low and a now / +1h / +2h strip.

Live markets

Two stock parts: price or daily change for any ticker Yahoo Finance knows, on the dial itself.

⚾

Live scores

Four game slots for pro baseball, football, basketball, hockey and the major soccer leagues including J1. Score and status, your team, updated while the game is on.

Privacy

What we keep, and why

Face Composer stores your dials, your photos and your settings on our server under your watch's Device ID, because that is how the watch fetches them. There is no account and no password. Prices come from Yahoo Finance, scores from public sports feeds, forecasts from Open-Meteo. Payment is handled by Stripe; we receive your email and Device ID to send the key and to contact you if needed.
