Concept

Run together, even when apart

Garmin's LiveTrack and GroupTrack focus on routes. PulseLink focuses on the people — see your friends' pulse, pace and effort in real time, and cheer them on with template messages, all from your wrist.

❤️

Live Heart Rate

See each member's current HR with a color-coded zone indicator. Privacy-friendly: each user can toggle HR sharing off independently.

👣

Steps & Distance

Track each member's daily steps and active distance. Compare effort across the group at a glance.

💬

Template Messages

Send pre-written cheers like 'Nice pace' or 'Hydrate' with one tap. Up to 20 customizable slots, addressed to everyone or one member.

🔐

Invite-Only Rooms

Host creates a private room with an 8-character invite code. New joins require host approval. Kick/ban tools for unwanted members.

⏱️

Adjustable Update Rate

Host chooses how often the room refreshes: 10s / 30s / 60s / 5min / Manual. Faster updates = more battery, slower = more endurance.

🛡️

3-Layer Privacy Control

Pause sharing entirely (full stop), set Manual interval (no auto-send), or toggle just HR off. You stay in control of what you share.

How it works

One host, up to 7 guests

Designed for small running crews. Host creates the room, guests join with a code, everyone sees everyone in real time.

1

Host Creates

One member subscribes and creates a room. An 8-character invite code is generated automatically.

2

Guests Join

Guests register a free account and enter the invite code. Host approves each new join request.

3

Start Running

Each watch sends HR / steps / distance on the host-chosen interval. The member list updates live on every wrist.

4

Cheer Each Other

Tap a template message to send a cheer or warning. Long-press a member to address them directly.

Activation

How to activate

1

Subscribe

Complete payment via Stripe. Your activation email will be sent immediately.

2

Sign In on Watch

Open Garmin Connect IQ → PulseLink → Settings → enter your email and password.

3

Customize Messages

Edit your 20 template messages from the same Settings screen. Empty slots are skipped on the watch.

4

Start Sharing

Save settings and restart PulseLink on your Garmin. Pick Host to create your room, or Guest to enter an invite code.

Subscription & Cancellation

Cancel anytime

Cancellation Policy: PulseLink Host is an annual subscription that renews automatically. You can cancel anytime via the Stripe Customer Portal — link is included in your activation email and Stripe receipt. Your hosting access continues until the end of the current billing period.

After cancellation or trial expiry, your account is downgraded to a free Guest account. Your owned room is deleted, but your account, settings and guest memberships remain — you can re-subscribe anytime to host again.

Refund Policy: As PulseLink is a digital service activated by account credentials, refunds are not provided once the activation email has been sent. If you experience activation issues, please contact and we'll resolve them promptly.

Privacy

Privacy & Health Data

PulseLink processes heart rate data, which is treated as a special category of personal data under GDPR. We collect only what is necessary: email, password (hashed), nickname, and the metrics you choose to share within your room.

You can stop sharing at any time using the in-app Pause toggle, switch to Manual interval (no auto-send), or turn off just heart rate sharing while keeping steps/distance active. You can delete your account and all associated data from this page at any time.
