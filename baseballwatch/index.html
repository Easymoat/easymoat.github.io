Concept

Follow your team — or your players

Pick your favorite team and BaseballWatch auto-follows the whole lineup and pitching staff — live batter, pitcher, count, score, and standings. Prefer to follow specific stars? Player Mode tracks up to 20 individuals across any team, throughout the entire game lifecycle.

🏟️

Team Mode

Pick your favorite team and BaseballWatch automatically tracks its entire lineup and pitching staff. The live view stays on the current batter and pitcher, with division standings just a tap away — the easiest way to follow your team every game.

⚾

Player Mode

Prefer to follow specific stars? Switch to Player Mode and register up to 20 individual players. Your watch is organized around them — the players you care about, front and center, whoever they play for.

🌐

Cross-Team Following

In Player Mode, track players from any team in any league. Build a cross-team roster only you would put together.

🕐

Full Game Lifecycle

Before the game (start time), during the game (live BSO and score), and after the game (final result). One screen, the entire day — in either mode.

Features

Real-time delivery, ready when you are

Real-time event notifications, a clean live view built for the small screen on your wrist — and coverage that reaches all the way down to the minor leagues.

🔔

Real-Time Event Notifications

Hits, home runs, stolen bases, walks, RBIs, strikeouts, runs allowed and more — pushed the moment they happen.

📣

On-Field Appearance Alerts

Get notified when your player takes the field — pitcher start, relief appearance, exit, or batter at-bat. Never miss a moment.

📊

Live Game View

See live score, base-out-strike count, and current pitcher / batter at a glance on your watch.

🎯

Pitch View

Tap the diamond on the live screen to open a pitch-by-pitch view of the current at-bat. Every pitch is plotted on the strike zone and color-coded by ball, strike, foul, or in-play, with the latest one ringed — plus pitch type, speed, the count, and runners on base. Tap the pitcher or batter to switch focus. (Major League and Triple-A.)

📺

On Your Wrist, Instantly

At the ballpark or watching the broadcast at home, a glance at your wrist gives you the live count, score, and your player's stats — no reaching for your phone, no missing the next pitch.

🔍

Browse Mode

Peek at any player in the current game — step through the batting order or pitching order and review each one's at-bat history, while the live score keeps updating in the background.

📈

Season Stats

Up-to-date season totals — AVG, HR, RBI, OPS for batters; W/L, ERA, K, WHIP, SV for pitchers.

🌱

Minor Leagues, Too

Player Mode isn't limited to the majors. Register a player from Triple-A, Double-A, High-A or Single-A and follow them exactly the same way — live at-bats, season stats, inning history. A level badge (AAA, AA …) appears on screen, and the detail screen names their parent club. Follow a prospect all the way up to the show.

⌚

Glance & Quick Resume

A two-line glance in the widget carousel shows your team's live score and your pinned player's day — tap it to jump straight into the game. The app remembers where you left off, too: the game list reopens right where you were, and one START press returns you to the last game you were watching.
