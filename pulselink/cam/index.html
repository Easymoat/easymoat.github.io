PulseLink Cam

Turn a spare iPhone into a watch-face camera. A photo is taken and sent to your watch every 3 minutes.

1. Connect your watch

Open PulseLink on your watch, then You menu > Link up > Device ID. Enter the code shown there. No account needed.

2. Camera

iPhone: if iOS keeps asking for camera access, open Settings > Apps > Safari > Camera and choose Allow. Otherwise Safari asks again every time and capture stops until you tap OK.

16 colors transfers to the watch about twice as fast. 32 and 64 cost the same (both use 8 bits per pixel) — so pick 16 for speed, or 64 for looks. If the image doesn't show on the watch, choose 16.

Keep this page open in Safari with the iPhone plugged in. The page keeps the screen awake. Photos go only to your own PulseLink account; on the watch, choose You menu → Image slot → Camera.
