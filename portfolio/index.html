WebMemo

Your portfolio workspace on the web

More room to think than a watch screen. WebMemo turns your holdings into a clear picture — and it's free for MarketWatch owners.

🗂️

Multiple portfolios

Organize by account, strategy or theme — up to 20 portfolios, each with its own symbols and cash.

📈

Live quotes & charts

Auto-refreshing prices for stocks, ETFs, FX, crypto and indices, with multi-period charts from 5-minute to monthly.

💰

Holdings & P&L

Record cost basis and quantity per symbol, add cash positions, and see evaluation value and profit/loss in your home currency.

🎯

Allocation & rebalance

Set a target allocation across asset classes and see how far your holdings drift — a calculator for your own plan.

🪙

Dividends

Trailing-12-month dividend income per symbol and across the portfolio, so you can see your yield at a glance.

🔬

Analysis

Correlation matrix, benchmark comparison and index sensitivity (beta) — educational tools to understand your mix.
