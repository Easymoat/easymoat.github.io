Features

Everything you need on your wrist

MarketWatch brings auto-refreshing market data to your Garmin with a clean, high-visibility display.

🕐

Watch Face Home

The app opens to a customizable watch face — analog Hands, Rim marks, or Digital clock, with optional Clock Ticks and your choice of hand, clock and background colors. Place up to 5 info rows (portfolio, a stock, Weather or Physical Bar), then tap to enter the markets list.

🖼️

Custom Watch Face Image

Upload your own image from the web and it appears as a circular image on your watch face. Just enter your Device ID — no account needed. Available during the free trial and after purchase.

📈

Auto-Refreshing Prices

Auto-refreshing quotes for stocks, crypto, FX & indices. Configurable 30sec–30min intervals.

※ Data from Yahoo Finance. May be delayed up to 15 min.

📊

Multi-Period Charts

Switch between 6 periods — 5-minute (today), 30-minute (1 week), 90-minute (1 month), daily (3mo), weekly (1y) and monthly (5y) — with high/low, date range, and a volume bar graph.

🎯

Price-Cross Alerts

Set up to 2 price-cross alerts per symbol. Get notified with vibration and sound when current price crosses your target.

📝

Position Memo

Track cost basis, holdings, evaluation amount, and P&L (amount + %) per symbol. Open the memo in the detail screen, then long-press a field to edit it on the watch.

⚙️

20 Custom Symbols

Fully configurable via the Connect IQ app. Mix stocks, crypto, FX, and indices freely.

⛅

Weather Bar

An optional on-watch bar showing temperature, humidity, wind speed & direction, air pressure and altitude — from Garmin weather and on-device sensors.

❤️

Physical Bar

An optional bar with live body stats right on the list screen: heart rate, floors climbed, weight, steps and calories.

🔋

Left & Right Rim Meters

Curved gauges on the left and right edge of the screen. Assign Body Battery, Stress, or device Battery to each side independently.

🌤️

Tap a Bar for Details

Tap the Weather or Physical bar to open full screens: current weather, hourly and daily forecast, altitude and barometer; heart rate, Body Battery, stress, steps and floors.

🔅

Battery Saver

After a few seconds without input the screen dims to just the clock, saving power. Raise your wrist or tap to return to exactly where you were.

🔔

Push Notifications

Optional Pushover integration — receive earnings and dividend alerts as push notifications on your phone.

Activation

How to activate

⚠️ Before you buy: check your Device ID

Your license key is generated from your watch's Device ID (format XXXX-XXXX), so the ID you enter at checkout must exactly match your watch. A wrong or completely different number means the key will not activate.

Where to find it: open MarketWatch. If the Watch Face home is off, long-press the clock area at the bottom of the list to open the Menu and set Watchface to On. Then, on the Watch Face screen, long-press an empty area to open the Watch Face menu, scroll to Device ID, and enter it exactly.
