Custom Compass Image

Put your own emblem at the center of the PulseLink compass — a family crest, team logo, or any symbol you love. Shown on watches with an active host license.

What makes an ideal image

  • Black background is best. The compass screen is black, so a black-background image blends in seamlessly — no visible edges. Transparent PNG backgrounds also work (treated as black).
  • Square, 400×400 px or larger. It will be scaled down to about 240–300 px on the watch. Non-square images are center-cropped.
  • Bright, high-contrast colors. White, yellow, cyan and red pop on the black screen. Dark colors sink into the background.
  • Photos work too. To fit the watch's memory the image is reduced to a limited color palette, so very subtle shades and smooth gradients may shift slightly. With enough colors, photos can still come through well. Logos, crests and bold shapes with flat colors are the most forgiving.

Will it show on your watch?

The more colors and the bigger the image, the more the watch has to handle. Use the guide below: green is fine, red may not show.

Simple tip: Standard works with any colors; for Full screen, 16 colors or fewer is the safe choice.

After uploading, restart PulseLink on your watch. The image downloads via your phone the next time the compass opens.
