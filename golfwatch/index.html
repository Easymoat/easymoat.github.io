The hole
Clock
Concept

See the hole, not just the score

A leaderboard tells you the number. GolfWatch shows you the fairway, the bunkers and the water your player is facing, and where the ball went.

⛳

The hole, on your wrist

Each hole's shape fills the screen, with par, yards and the wind turned to the direction of play: INTO, DOWN or CROSS.

🏌️

Every shot

New shots play as they come in. The ball flies in an arc, putts roll toward the cup, and each shot is described in a line of text. Replay a hole any time with START.

🎯

Around the green

The view moves in as the ball gets close, with the pin marked. Tap to switch between the whole hole, the second shot and the green.

Features

From the first tee to the last putt

Your players stay in your order, and the whole field is one tap away.

⭐

Your five players

Home lists your players in the order you choose, with position, score and where they are on the course. Add anyone from the Leaderboard with a long press.

🕐

18 holes around the clock

The clock screen turns the rim into the course. Your players are colored dots on the hole they are playing, with their score and shot, and the dot flashes when a new shot comes in.

🗺️

Course map

Tap the time to see the whole course, with your players on it. Zoom in up to x3 with a tap. At the course, turn on My position to add yourself.

📋

Course scorecard

All 18 holes on one screen, with strokes under each, birdies circled and bogeys boxed. Tap any hole to open it. Even if your players are not in this week's event, you can look over the course before it starts.

🏆

Leaderboard and field

The whole leaderboard with flags and your players highlighted. Before the event, the field, with each player's season stats and recent results.

📳

Shot alerts

Feel each shot of the player you have open: sound and vibration, vibration only, or off. When another of your players finishes a hole, a pop-up tells you the result.

🌤️

Weather at the course

Now, hourly and daily at the course, not where you are, including wind and gusts.

⌚

Glance and watch face

A two-line glance shows one player. Put a player's position and score in a data field of your watch face; it keeps updating about every 5 minutes, even with GolfWatch closed.

⏪

Past events

Open an event that has finished and go back through your players' rounds, shot by shot.

At the Course

At the event, see where you and your players are

Watching in person? Turn on My position in Settings, then tap the time on the clock to open the course map.

Course map with My position on (sample)
📍

See yourself on the map

A blue dot marks where you are, with your players as colored dots on the same map.

📏

How far to your player

The bottom line shows roughly how far you are from one of your players on the course.

🚶

Get there first

Player positions run about 1-2 minutes behind, so to catch a player, wait at the next hole.

🔒

Only while you look

GPS runs only while the map is on screen. Your position stays on the watch.

Player ID Lookup

Find your players

Enter a name, copy the ID, and paste it into Player slot 1 to 5 in the GolfWatch settings in Connect IQ. This works without the pass. Picking players from the list on the watch is for the trial and the pass.

🔍

Enter at least 2 letters, in English. Results come from ESPN, and what you type is sent to ESPN.

3

4

Enjoy

A key also arrives by email. You do not need to enter it if you entered your Device ID when you bought. If you do need it: Garmin Connect IQ → GolfWatch → Settings → License Key.

Privacy

Privacy

GolfWatch does not collect personal information beyond your Garmin Device ID and email address (used for the trial, activation and billing). When you enter a license key, the watch sends it with your Device ID to check it.

The players you register are stored on your watch. Their ESPN IDs are sent to our server to fetch their scores. If you turn on My position, your location is used only on the watch and is not sent anywhere.

Course maps are simplified drawings of our own and are for following the event. A map marked Being confirmed is still being checked. GolfWatch is an independent app, not affiliated with or endorsed by the PGA TOUR, ESPN or Garmin.
