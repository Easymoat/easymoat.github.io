MarketWatch Max & WebMemo — User Manual

Two companions to MarketWatch: a bigger portfolio view on the watch, and a workspace on the web. What each one does, and how they connect.

1. Getting Started

MarketWatch is the paid Garmin app: up to 3 portfolios of 20 symbols each, live prices, charts, alerts and a watch-face home. Two companions come with it at no extra cost.

  • MarketWatch Max — a second Garmin app for bigger portfolios: up to 7 portfolios of 20 symbols each, a portfolio overview home and a Markets list. It unlocks with your MarketWatch license key.
  • WebMemo — a web page (app.easymoat.com/webmemo/) where you build and analyze portfolios on a big screen: holdings, trades, allocation, dividends and analysis. You sign in with your MarketWatch Device ID and license key. It works from the first day of a new MarketWatch trial: the key is shown on the watch during the trial, and it comes by email when you buy.

One purchase covers all three. Nothing is charged for Max or WebMemo, and there is no subscription.

2. MarketWatch Max

Max shows the same market data as MarketWatch, with the same detail screens and the same controls. This chapter covers what is different. For charts, technical overlays, the position memo, the Net detail and alerts, use the MarketWatch manual.

Unlocking Max

  1. Install MarketWatch Max from the Connect IQ Store. On its own it shows a License Required screen.
  2. In the Connect IQ app on your phone, open the settings of MarketWatch Max and enter your MarketWatch license key in License Key. After a purchase it is the key from the confirmation email. During a new MarketWatch trial it is shown on the watch: open the Device ID screen of MarketWatch and copy the two lines under Key for WebMemo / Max.
  3. Open Max. It is now linked to your MarketWatch and uses the same account, so the portfolios you choose in WebMemo appear here.
MarketWatch must be purchased or in a new 14-day trial. One Max can be linked per key; a second watch with the same key will not unlock. If you move to a new watch, email easymoat@gmail.com and we move the link for you. Once linked, Max keeps working without entering the key again.

What Max adds

  • Up to 7 portfolios — each with up to 20 symbols, 140 in all. Which portfolios are on the watch is chosen in WebMemo (chapter 4). MarketWatch carries up to 3.
  • Portfolio overview home — Max opens on a list of your portfolios with each one's aggregate P&L. Scroll to a portfolio and open it to see its symbols.
  • Markets — a read-only list of up to 20 indices and FX rates, chosen in WebMemo (the symbols you mark for sync there, with the names you gave them) and brought over with Markets Down (chapter 4). Each has its own detail screen. Prices refresh while it is open, and Refresh in the portfolio list updates it too.
  • Price move alerts — symbols that reach the ±% you set in Markets on the web are highlighted, and the Markets row in the menu blinks yellow while one is alerting.
  • Net detail per portfolio — the day's move, the aggregate P&L chart with its break-even line, and the Contribution screen that splits today's move by symbol, exactly as in MarketWatch.

The detail screen

Same as MarketWatch: chart with Per: (window) and TF: (bar size), high/low, S:/E:, Vol:, volume bars, technical overlays and the position memo. Prices are shown with the decimals of their currency (yen and won as whole numbers, dollars to two decimals).

3. WebMemo

WebMemo runs in any browser at app.easymoat.com/webmemo/, on a computer, a tablet or a phone in landscape. There is no account to create: your MarketWatch Device ID is the account.

Signing in

  • Device ID and key — enter the Device ID shown in MarketWatch and your license key. After a purchase the key is in the confirmation email. During a new 14-day trial it is on the watch: the Device ID screen of MarketWatch shows it in two lines under Key for WebMemo / Max. The watch receives this key only once, when the trial starts. It stays on that screen for the rest of the trial, but reinstalling the app erases it, so note it down.
  • Demo — open app.easymoat.com/webmemo/?demo=1 to try every screen on sample data without signing in.

Portfolios and holdings

  • Up to 20 portfolios — each with up to 20 symbols and its own cash. Organize by account, strategy or theme.
  • Memo tabs — every symbol has a Memo tab (cost basis, quantity, alert prices), a Trades tab, and any tabs you add for your own notes.
  • Trades tab — record buys and sells and the holdings, average cost and cash update themselves.
  • Charts — pick a period and the bar size is shown with it, for example 1D · 5-minute bars, the same periods as on the watch.

Planning and analysis

  • Allocation and rebalance — set a target across asset classes and see how far your holdings have drifted from it. A calculator for your own plan, not advice.
  • Dividends — trailing-12-month income per symbol and for the whole portfolio.
  • Analysis — a correlation matrix against market benchmarks you choose from Japan, the US, Europe, Asia and FX, a correlation ranking, benchmark comparison and index sensitivity (beta).
  • Economic calendar — market-moving releases grouped by day, with importance, consensus, previous and actual values.
  • CSV — export and import your data, to back it up or move it between browsers.
Your data is kept under your Device ID only. Delete all my data at the bottom of the page removes everything at once. See the privacy policy for details.

4. Working Together

WebMemo is where you decide what each watch shows. The watches then pull it in, and what you edit on a watch can go back to WebMemo.

Choosing what goes to each watch

  • MWM chip — each portfolio in WebMemo has an MWM chip. Turn it on for up to 7 portfolios and those are the ones MarketWatch Max shows.
  • MW chip — the same for MarketWatch, for up to 3 portfolios.
  • Markets — in the Markets view, mark up to 20 indices and FX rates for sync. They become the Markets list in Max, with the names and ±% alerts you set, the next time you run Markets Down on the watch.

Two-way sync

Both directions are started from the watch, so you decide when each side is updated. In Max, open Menu at the bottom of the portfolio list, then Sync: WebMemo Down brings the portfolios you chose on the web to the watch, WebMemo Up sends the watch's current portfolios and memos to the web, and Markets Down brings the Markets list. In MarketWatch the same two items are called WebMemo Load and WebMemo Save. Cost basis, quantity and alert prices you edit on the watch reach WebMemo with the next Up, and edits made on the web reach the watch with the next Down. Nothing syncs on its own.

MarketWatch and Max are linked through the same license key, so they share one account: the portfolios and memos you see in WebMemo are the ones behind both watches.

5. Troubleshooting

Max shows License Required

Enter your MarketWatch license key under License Key in the Connect IQ settings of Max. If it is already there, check that MarketWatch is purchased or in a new trial, and that no other watch is linked with the same key.

I changed my watch and Max will not unlock

One Max is linked per key. Email easymoat@gmail.com with your new Device ID and we move the link.

WebMemo will not sign me in

Use the Device ID shown in MarketWatch (not the one from another Easymoat app) with the matching key. Where the key is: in the confirmation email after a purchase, or on the Device ID screen of MarketWatch (Key for WebMemo / Max) during a new trial. The key is not shown during a 7-day re-trial, nor for a trial started on an older version of MarketWatch; in those cases WebMemo and Max open after the purchase. If the key is gone (for example after reinstalling the app), email easymoat@gmail.com. When a trial ends without a purchase, WebMemo and Max stop too.

The numbers on the watch and the web differ

Nothing syncs on its own: run WebMemo Down or WebMemo Up (Max) or WebMemo Load or WebMemo Save (MarketWatch) on the watch when you want the two sides to match. Totals also differ by design in mixed-currency portfolios: the watch adds raw values without converting currencies, while WebMemo converts foreign holdings to yen.

Anything else: easymoat@gmail.com. Please include your Device ID.
