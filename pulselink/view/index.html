Dark photos are auto-enhanced on the server: boosted, denoised and rendered in night-vision green (16 shades = the fastest transfer tier). Auto decides per photo by its brightness. Total darkness still shows black — a small night light works wonders.

16 colors reaches the watch about twice as fast. 32 and 64 cost the same, so choose 64 if you want the best looking image. This page always shows full quality.

Changes reach the camera within about 10-30 seconds, and a fresh photo is taken right away. While you are adjusting, this page refreshes every 5 seconds for about 3 minutes (adjustment mode).
