Sea
Watch

Tides, swell, wind and water temperature for the spot you are heading to, on your Garmin. Pick a fishing pier or a surf break on the map of the US Pacific coast and Hawaii, and see what the sea is doing without taking out your phone.

Price and supported watches will be announced at launch.

Concept

From the whole coast to your spot

Start from the map of the US, open your region, and zoom in step by step until you are looking at the pier or the break itself.

🗺️

Zoom in to the spot

Each step brings you closer: the region, the stretch of coast with wind arrows over the sea, then aerial photos of the spot.

🎣

Fishing and surf spots

Piers, jetties, rocks and beaches for fishing, and surf breaks, chosen from published lists. Pick fishing or surf, and the map shows the alerts that matter for it.

📍

Where you are

Turn on My location to see yourself on the map and how far it is to the spot. Your position stays on the watch.

Features

Everything you check before heading out

Open a spot and the tide, the swell and the wind are on one screen.

🌊

Tides for two weeks

The next high or low, the tide now and whether it is rising, today's tide curve, and the highs and lows for the next 14 days.

🏄

Swell, wind and water

Swell height and period, wind, water temperature and the weather at the spot.

🌅

Sun and moon

Sunrise and sunset, the moon phase and the tide range for the day.

🕐

Spot time or your time

Times are shown in the spot's local time. Tap the time to switch to your watch's time, handy when you are planning from another time zone.

⚠️

Alerts on the map

National Weather Service alerts are marked on the map: high surf, beach hazards such as rip currents, small craft, gale and storm, coastal flood and marine information. Tsunami alerts always show.

🦠

Red tide forecast

Along the California coast, the map can show NOAA's harmful algal bloom forecast as a band of color.

☁️

Clouds and currents

See the cloud cover over your region, and tidal current arrows where they are available.

⌚

Glance and watch face

The glance lists the coming highs and lows. Put the next high or low, swell, water temperature or wind in a data field on your watch face. Both show the last spot you opened.

⬇️

One region at a time

Download the region you go to, through your phone. The watch keeps one region at a time; choosing another one replaces it.

Before you go

Please note

Forecasts and alerts in SeaWatch are for reference. Always check official forecasts and warnings and the conditions on site before you go out. SeaWatch is not for navigation.

SeaWatch is an independent app, not affiliated with or endorsed by NOAA, the National Weather Service or Garmin.
