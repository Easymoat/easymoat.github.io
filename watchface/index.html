3CollectionSend the dial to the watch and keep finished dials
Sync to watch sends the dial above (layout + the photo as framed) to the watch. Save to collection keeps that same dial here as one item, so it stays available even after you change the dial or the photos. Each item can be loaded back or sent to the watch.
