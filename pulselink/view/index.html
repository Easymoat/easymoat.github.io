Remote control

Setting the camera up? Setup mode shoots every 30 seconds for 5 minutes so you can check framing and brightness quickly on this page. It ends by itself and goes back to the normal 3-minute interval — press it again to extend.

Torch is not available on this camera phone — the web torch API needs iOS 17.5+ or Android.

Dark photos are auto-enhanced on the server: boosted, denoised and rendered in night-vision green (16 shades = the fastest transfer tier). Auto decides per photo by its brightness. Total darkness still shows black — a small night light works wonders.

16 colors reaches the watch about twice as fast. 32 and 64 cost the same, so choose 64 if you want the best looking image. This page always shows full quality.

Changes reach the camera within about 10-30 seconds, and a fresh photo is taken right away. For rapid framing checks, use Setup mode — it is the only control that changes the shooting interval.
