Face Composer & The Face — User Manual

Build a Garmin watch face on the web and wear it within 5 minutes. Every screen, part and setting.

1. Getting Started

Face Composer is a web page where you build your Garmin watch face. The Face is the watch face that shows it on your wrist. There is no account and no password: your watch's Device ID is the only thing that connects the two.

  1. Install The Face from the Connect IQ Store and choose it as your watch face.
  2. Look at the bottom of the dial. A small line shows a build code and your Device ID, four characters, a dash and four more (like a1b2-3456). If you do not see it, turn on Show diagnostics in The Face's settings (chapter 2).
  3. On a computer, open easymoat.com/watchface/ and type the ID into 5 Watch → Device ID. It is kept in that browser, so you type it once.
  4. Build your dial: place parts, pick a photo, choose colors and sizes (chapters 4 to 7).
  5. In 4 Collection, press Save to collection, then press To watch on the saved item. The watch shows it within 5 minutes.
Treat your Device ID like a house key. Whoever types it into Face Composer can send a dial to your watch, so share it only with people you trust. Face Composer asks you to confirm before the first send to a watch.

2. The Face on Your Watch

Right after you install it, The Face shows the Lobby dial: classic hands, the date, battery, body data, weather, two stocks and two games. The first dial you send from Face Composer replaces it.

How the watch gets your dial

  • Every 5 minutes: The Face checks for a new dial in the background. There is no cable and nothing to press on the watch.
  • One dial at a time: Sending another dial replaces the one on the watch.
  • Through your phone: The watch reaches the internet through Garmin Connect on your phone. Without it, The Face keeps showing the last dial it received.
  • Live data: Forecasts, stock prices and scores refresh with each check. A forecast older than 3 hours is dropped and the watch's own Garmin weather is used instead.

The diagnostics line

Two small lines at the bottom of the dial show the build code with your Device ID, and the result of the last check. They are hidden in low-power mode, and you can turn them off with Show diagnostics once you no longer need the ID.

Hold for details

With the Hold for details setting on (the default), touching and holding a part opens a detail screen. Holding an empty area opens the watch face menu as usual.

HoldOpens
Weather partsThe weather detail screens below. With Weather detail set to Garmin, the watch's own weather screen instead.
Battery, heart rate, steps, floors, calories, Body Battery, stress, respiration, altitude, notifications, and the graphsThe watch's own detail screen for that metric.

Weather detail screens

  • Hourly: the next 5 hours: time, weather, temperature, wind and chance of rain.
  • Daily: 5 days: weather, high, low and chance of rain.
  • Now: a large icon and temperature, today's high and low, humidity, wind, chance of rain and pressure.

Hold again to go to the next page. After the last page you are back on the dial.

Settings in the Connect IQ app

Open the Connect IQ app on your phone, choose The Face, then Settings.

  • Show diagnostics: the two lines at the bottom with your Device ID and the last check. On by default.
  • Hold for details: hold a part to open its detail screen. On by default.
  • Credits: the icon license (read only).

On AMOLED watches in low-power mode, the Seconds part is not drawn and Time + sec shows only the time. Everything comes back when you raise your wrist.

3. The Face Composer Screen

Face Composer is made for a computer screen. On a phone it opens a smaller screen for switching dials (chapter 10).

  • Parts (left): every part you can place, in groups. Click a group heading to fold it.
  • 1 Layout: the device you are designing for, and the settings of the part you selected.
  • 2 Dial: the dial itself, where you move parts and the picture.
  • 3 Image: the photo and the shape it is cut to.
  • 4 Collection: your saved dials and the To watch button.
  • 5 Watch: your Device ID, where the weather comes from, and your Pass.
  • Right panel: background, sizes, style, stocks, games and the list of parts on the dial.

Choose your watch in 1 Layout → Device first, so the dial has the right size and fonts. Your work is kept in this browser as you go, and comes back when you reopen the page.

4. Placing and Editing Parts

Adding parts

  • Drag: a part from the palette onto the dial. It snaps to the grid. Let go outside the dial to cancel.
  • Click: a part in the palette. It lands near the center, in a free space.
  • Hands and rims: click to put on, click again to take off. One set of hands at a time; rims can be stacked two deep.

Selecting and moving

  • Click: a part on the dial, or its row in the Elements list on the right.
  • Shift / ⌘ / Ctrl + click: add or remove parts from the selection.
  • Drag an empty area: draw a box to select every part it touches. When the dial has a picture, hold Shift, ⌘ or Ctrl while you drag (a plain drag moves the picture). Hands and rims are not boxed.
  • Arrow keys: move the selection one grid step.
  • Delete / Backspace: remove the selection. Esc clears it.
  • ⌘Z / Ctrl+Z: undo, up to 60 steps. ⇧⌘Z or Ctrl+Y redoes. The Undo button does not undo picture changes.

Selected part

  • Anchor: Center, Left or Right: which edge of the text stays put when the value grows.
  • Size: S, M, L or XL (graphs: Small or Large).
  • Icon size: Auto, or a size of its own.
  • Show: Icon + value, Icon only or Value only.
  • Arrange: Icon left, Icon right or Icon above.
  • Color: the picker, plus the five colors you used last. A dial can use up to 22 colors.
  • Align: Same X or Same Y lines up several selected parts with the one you clicked last.
  • Front / Back: draw the part over or under the others.
  • Duplicate / Remove: copy the part one grid step below, or delete it.

Dial tools

  • Grid: 200 × 200 down to 10 × 10. Show turns the lines on and off; Dim and Bright set how strong they are.
  • Parked: drag a part here to take it off the dial while you arrange the rest. Parked parts are not saved and do not go to the watch.
  • Hide parts / Hide image: look at the picture or the parts alone. Nothing changes.
  • Clear dial: empties the dial and resets the background and sizes, after asking. Undo brings it back.

Limits

The watch takes 40 parts. Lines and rectangles do not count (up to 60 lines). Above 32 parts the count turns amber: the watch draws more slowly.

Start the dial over

At the bottom of the page. These replace the parts on the dial; nothing is sent to the watch and your Collection does not change.

  • Start from Lobby: the dial The Face ships with.
  • Start from Minimal: three parts: time, date and battery.
  • Take every part off: an empty dial. The background, text sizes and picture stay.

Hands and rim

  • Hands: Bars, Needles, Kite, Outline, Rim marks, Classic, Outline round.
  • Rim: Ticks, Dots, 12 3 6 9, Ring and Batons. Position: Inner or Edge.
  • Meter 1 to 4: arcs around the edge, each one measuring battery, steps, Body Battery, stress, humidity or rain today. Each arc has its own on/off, color and track color; right-click a color to set it back to automatic.

Lines and shapes

  • Line: Direction (│ ─ ╲ ╱), Length, Thickness, and Joint: R rounds the corner where two lines meet (both need R) or Sharp.
  • Circle: Diameter, Thickness, Shape (Full, Half, Quarter), Turn in 45° steps, Fill (full circles only), and Meter: the ring can fill up with battery, steps, Body Battery, stress, humidity or rain today.
  • Rectangle: Width, Height, Thickness and Join (R or Sharp).

Markets (right panel)

Stock 1 and Stock 2 show a price; the % versions show the day's change. Drop one on the dial, then fill in Selected part:

  • Symbol: as Yahoo Finance writes it: AAPL, 7203.T for Tokyo, ^GSPC for an index, BTC-USD for crypto. Face Composer looks it up and shows the price, or not found.
  • Name: your own label, up to 12 letters and digits.
  • Value / Label: Price or Change %; show the Symbol or your Name.
  • Up / down: Keep color, or By move: green or red by direction (which is up is set in Style).

Games (right panel)

There are four game slots, Game 1 to Game 4. USA Sports (MLB, NFL, NBA, NHL) and Soccer (Premier League, LaLiga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, J1 League) are the same four slots, so Game 1 from either group is the same slot. Each part is a score or a status line.

  • Game N: the league. Changing it asks you to pick the team again.
  • Team: your team.
  • State: Keep color, or By state: blue at the top of an inning, red at the bottom, green in play, orange when final, your color before the start.

When your team has no game today, the slot shows its next game within 7 days, or -- when there is none (for example out of season).

7. Photos and Shapes

Adding a photo

  • Add photo…: pick a PNG, JPEG or WebP. It is stored at up to 1024 pixels on the long side. Up to 10 photos.
  • Use it: click a photo to put it on the dial; click it again to take it off.
  • Delete photo: removes it from your photos. Dials already saved from it keep their cut-out but can no longer be re-adjusted.

Framing

  • Dragging the dial moves: Picture moves the photo inside the shape; Frame moves the shape.
  • Size, Zoom: the size of the shape (30 to 100%) and of the photo inside it (1 to 3×).
  • Brightness, Saturation: tone the photo. Reset tone puts them back.
  • Reset frame / Reset image: re-center the shape at 100%, or start the picture over. Nothing is deleted.

Shape

Square, Circle, Half circle, Heart, Diamond, Spade, Club, Star, Triangle, Pentagon or Hexagon. Shift+click adds a second shape, and Adjust chooses which one you are editing. Rotate turns it in 45° steps. Fill color paints the shape one color instead of using a photo.

Colors on the watch

2, 4, 8, 16 or 32 colors (the watch shows at most 32). Next to it, Face Composer shows the picture's size on the watch: OK, borderline or too big. Fewer colors make a lighter picture; logos and flat artwork look good with very few.

  • Auto background: on, it picks the dial's background color from the photo, from Deep to Pale.
  • PulseLink camera: for people who also use PulseLink, another Easymoat app that can turn a spare phone into a fixed camera. With Camera on and your PulseLink ID, the latest camera picture fills the shape.

Garmin watches have no transparency. Where a shape's edge meets the background, Face Composer blends the two into solid colors, so the preview and the watch match.

8. Collection and To Watch

  • Save to collection: keeps the dial and its picture as an item. The name is optional (Item N otherwise). Up to 30 items.
  • To watch <ID>: sends the item to the watch whose ID is on the button. It replaces whatever that watch shows, within 5 minutes.
  • Showing: marks the item that is on the watch now.
  • Edit / Delete: bring the item back into the editor, or remove it.

The first time you send in a session, and after you change the Device ID, Face Composer asks you to confirm the watch. Parked parts are not saved; the save message tells you if any were left out.

Your watch is showing the dial you sentDone. It also tells you when the watch last checked.
Your watch has not checked for …The watch may be off or away from your phone.

B · Where the weather parts get their forecast

  • Use this computer's place: asks this browser where you are and sends it, rounded to about 11 km. Opening the page updates it, so it follows you when you travel.
  • Follow PulseLink: if PulseLink runs on the same watch, type its ID and the forecast follows the watch wherever it goes.
  • Use the watch's own GPS: stops sending a place from here. The watch then has to find its position itself, which can be slow or fail indoors.

Pass

The Pass line shows your trial or Pass: days left, the date it is valid until, or that it has ended, with a link to buy or renew. Pass key appears when you hold a Pass; it is used for sharing, which is coming soon. Saving and To watch work without it.

10. On Your Phone

  • Sign in: type your Device ID once. It stays on the phone.
  • Switch dials: press To watch on any item. Delete works too; editing needs a computer.
  • Change / Editor: Change switches to another watch. Editor opens the full editor if you really need it.

11. Trial and Pass

  • 14 days free: every feature, from your first save. No card, no account.
  • 1-Year Pass: $9.99, one payment, valid for 365 days from the day you buy. No subscription and no auto-renewal.
  • Buying: on the Face Composer page. Enter The Face's Device ID at checkout (not the ID of another Easymoat app; each app has its own). The key is emailed to you.
  • Renewing: buy again with the same Device ID whenever you like. 365 days are added to what is left, so renewing early loses nothing.

When the trial or Pass ends

  • Face Composer: still opens. You can look at your dials and delete them, but saving to Collection and To watch are off until you buy or renew.
  • The Face: keeps showing the last dial you sent, with its live data. Nothing on the watch stops.

A watch that has held a Pass does not start a new free trial when it ends.

12. Supported Devices

Face Composer lists every supported watch in 1 Layout → Device. There are two levels of support:

  • Full (20 watches): AMOLED watches with vector fonts: every text size, Bold and the photo work exactly as you compose them. Forerunner 165, 265, 265s, 570, 965 and 970; fēnix 8 (AMOLED), 8 Pro and E; Venu 3, 3S, 4 and X1; vívoactive 6; D2 Mach 2.
  • Partial (42 watches): placement, colors and icons work; text comes in fixed sizes. Includes epix Gen 2, fēnix 7 series, Venu 2 family, vívoactive 5, Instinct 3, Approach, MARQ Gen 2, Descent and Forerunner 255 / 955.

Photos

  • Connect IQ 5.0 watches: Venu 2, Venu 2 Plus, Venu 2S, Venu Sq 2, Venu Sq 2 Music and D2 Air X10 cannot show the photo. The dial appears without it.
  • MIP screens: fēnix 7, Forerunner 255 / 955, Enduro and other memory-in-pixel watches show 256 colors (Instinct E and Instinct 3 Solar: black and white). Photos break up; logos and flat shapes work well.

Instinct 3 Solar 45 mm and Instinct E 40 / 45 mm have very little memory for watch faces. Before you send a dial they show a simpler starting dial, and their icons are drawn more simply.

On MIP watches the Seconds part is redrawn every second, and any part placed over it disappears each time. Keep seconds clear of other parts.

13. Troubleshooting

The dial does not arrive

Check the line under Device ID in 5 Watch. The watch checks every 5 minutes, and only while The Face is your watch face and your phone is nearby with Garmin Connect running. Make sure the ID on the watch and in Face Composer are exactly the same.

I cannot see my Device ID on the watch

Turn on Show diagnostics in The Face's settings. The line is also hidden in low-power mode; raise your wrist.

Face Composer asks me to confirm the watch

It asks on the first send after you open the page or change the Device ID. Check the ID on the button and in the message before you press OK.

The weather is for the wrong place

Choose where the forecast comes from in 5 Watch → B. Watch faces often cannot get a position on their own, so Use this computer's place or Follow PulseLink is more reliable.

The text looks different on my watch

Your watch may be a partial-support model with fixed font sizes (chapter 12). Pick your exact watch in 1 Layout → Device to see it as the watch does.

A stock shows -- or not found

Check the symbol as Yahoo Finance writes it (7203.T, ^GSPC, BTC-USD).

A game slot shows --

Your team has no game today or in the next 7 days, often out of season.

The photo looks rough

Try more colors in 3 Image (up to 32). On MIP watches photos always break up; use a logo or a flat shape instead.

Holding a part opens nothing

Check that Hold for details is on and that your watch supports complications. Stocks need MarketWatch; Games 1 and 2 need BaseballWatch and MLB; Games 3 and 4 and other leagues open nothing.

I cannot reach the watch face menu

Hold an empty area of the dial, or turn off Hold for details.

Some parts are missing on the watch

Parked parts are not sent. The watch also takes at most 40 parts (lines do not count).

I deleted a photo and cannot re-adjust a dial

Dials keep their cut-out, but the original photo is gone. Add the photo again and save a new item.

Saving and To watch are off

Your trial or Pass has ended. Buy or renew from the link in 5 Watch; your dials and the watch are untouched.

Anything else: easymoat@gmail.com. Please include your Device ID and your watch model.
