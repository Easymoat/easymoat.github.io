GolfWatch — User Manual

Every screen, gesture and setting.

1. Getting Started

GolfWatch follows the week's PGA Tour event on your Garmin. Pick up to five players, and your wrist shows where each one is on the course, the hole they are playing, and every shot as it comes in.

  1. Install GolfWatch on your watch from the Connect IQ Store.
  2. Open the app. The clock screen comes first, with the event's name and the weather at the course.
  3. Press DOWN (or swipe), or tap the bottom of the screen, to see the list of players this week.
  4. Hold a player's row and choose a slot. They are now on your Home screen.
Scores, shots and maps reach your watch through your phone. Keep the watch paired with Garmin Connect Mobile and the phone online.

Supported watches: Forerunner 965, Forerunner 265, Venu 3, Venu 2S, Approach S70 42mm and fēnix 9 Pro 51mm.

The trial and the 1-Year Pass

Every feature is free for 14 days. The trial starts by itself when you open the app, with no payment and no account. After that, the 1-Year Pass costs $4.99 and lasts 365 days from the day you buy. It does not renew automatically.

Buying the pass

  1. On the watch, open Settings and find your Device ID. After the trial, the same ID also appears when you open a pass screen.
  2. Go to easymoat.com/golfwatch, open the purchase page and enter your Device ID: 9 characters including the hyphen (xxxx-xxxx).
  3. Open the app again. The pass is now active.

A key also arrives by email. You do not need to enter it if you entered your Device ID when you bought. If you do need it, enter it in Connect IQ → GolfWatch → Settings → License Key.

3. Clock Screen

The rim is the course: 18 equal parts, with hole 1 at 12 o'clock, the front nine on the right and the back nine on the left. Each of your players is a colored dot on the hole they are playing, with their score and shot beside it (tee, 1st, 2nd, putt). The dot flashes when a new shot comes in.

Players who have not started wait to the right of hole 1, and players who have finished sit to the left of hole 18. They appear as dots only. Playing partners share one dot.

Above the event name are the weather and wind at the course. Below the time are the date, then your own battery and steps. Clock style in Settings switches the time between Digital and Hands.

To start on Home instead, turn off Clock screen in Settings.

4. Course Map

Tap the time on the clock screen to see the whole course, north up. Each player has a dot and a label with their hole, shot and score.

Do thisWhat happens
TapZoom in on that spot, x2 then x3. At x3 a tap moves the center.
UP / DOWN (or swipe)Zoom in / out.
STARTZoom in one step. After x3, back to the whole course.
BACKZoom out one step. From the whole course, back to the clock.

Following a player around the course

The player dots are about 1–2 minutes behind.

  • A dot is where the player's last shot landed.
  • On the green a dot may pause for a few minutes. By then the player is finishing the hole and walking to the next tee.
  • A hole takes about 10–20 minutes, so to catch a player, wait at the next hole.

Your own position

Turn on My position in Settings to see yourself on the map, with the nearest hole and the distance to your first playing player, for example YOU H12 - Matsuyama 140y. GPS runs only while the map is on screen. Your position stays on the watch.

About the maps

We draw the course maps ourselves, and they are simplified. A map marked Being confirmed is still being checked and may change during the event. A course without a map shows No course map; scores and the leaderboard still work.

5. Home and Your Players

Home lists your players in slots 1 to 5, in the order you set. Each row shows position, the player's color, name, score to par, and on the right where they are: the last hole finished, today's strokes once they are done, or their tee time in your watch's local time.

Tap a player, or STARTOpen that player's Course.

UP / DOWN (or swipe)Move between rows. UP from the first row goes to the clock.
Tap the event nameOpen the event overview.
Tap the date at the bottomOpen the Leaderboard.
Tap the clock at the topOpen Settings.

Adding a player

On the watch: hold a row on the Leaderboard or Field and choose a slot. MENU does the same for the players on screen.

On your phone: open GolfWatch's settings in Connect IQ and enter the player's ESPN ID in Player slot 1 to 5. To find an ID, search by name in English on easymoat.com/golfwatch. This works without the pass. Picking from the list on the watch is for the trial and the pass.

GolfWatch starts with the top five on the 2025 money list: Scottie Scheffler, Tommy Fleetwood, Rory McIlroy, Russell Henley and J.J. Spaun. A player who is not in this week's event shows Not playing on Home.

6. Leaderboard and Field

Before the event the list is headed FIELD and shows who is playing. Once play starts it becomes the LEADERBOARD: position, flag, name, score, and the hole they have reached, today's strokes when finished, or their tee time. Your players are highlighted.

7. Course: the Scorecard

A player's round on one screen: a small picture of all 18 holes, the strokes under each, and the totals below. A yellow frame marks the hole they are on.

  • A circle is a birdie, a double circle an eagle or better.
  • A square is a bogey, a double square a double bogey or worse.

8. Hole Screen

The hole's shape fills the screen: fairway, bunkers, water, trees and green. On the left are the hole number, par and yards; on the right, the wind, turned to the direction of play and marked INTO, DOWN or CROSS.

New shots play as they come in: the ball flies in an arc, putts roll, and each shot is described at the bottom. The view moves in to the green for the short game. When the ball drops, the result appears: BIRDIE, PAR, BOGEY and so on. If a player you follow is in the same group, their shots are drawn too, in their own color.

The pin

Around the green, the top line tells you about the pin: PIN R2 means it is that round's position, PIN R2 EST means it is estimated and may be a few meters off, and NO PIN means it is not known yet.

Shot data not ready means the shots for this hole have not arrived. Some events do not provide shots at all; scores still work.

9. Alerts

Shot alert

While you have a player open, your watch tells you each time they hit a shot on the hole they are playing. Choose Sound and vibrate, Vibrate only (the default) or Off in Settings. Alerts come while the app is open.

Your other players

When another of your players finishes a hole, a pop-up appears for 5 seconds, for example TANAKA #7 BIRDIE -4. Tap it to open their Course, or ignore it and it goes away. Players in the same group as the one you are watching do not pop up, because you can already see them.

10. Event Overview and Past Events

Tap the event name on Home to see the course, location, dates, par and yards, purse, defending champion, current round and weather.

Tap PAST EVENTS at the bottom, or press MENU, to choose an event that has finished. Home then shows that event, with PAST beside the course name, and you can open your players' rounds and replay their shots. Choose Current event to come back.

11. Weather at the Course

Tap or hold the weather or wind on the clock screen. There are three pages: now, hourly and daily. UP / DOWN (or swipe) changes the page.

  • Now: temperature, chance of rain, high and low, wind and gusts.
  • Hourly: four hours at a time, up to 12 hours. Tap for the next four.
  • Daily: four days at a time, up to 7 days.

12. Glance and Watch Face

Glance

The glance shows one player on two lines: name, then position, score and status. It shows what the app last loaded, so open the app to refresh it. Choose the player with Glance player in Settings. If they are not playing this week, the next slot takes over. Opening from the glance goes straight to that player's Course.

Complication

Add GolfWatch player to a data field on your watch face to see a player's position and score. It keeps updating about every 5 minutes, even with GolfWatch closed. Hold the field to open that player's Course. Choose the player with Complication in Settings.

After the trial, the glance and the watch face keep showing your player. Holding the field opens Home instead of the player's Course until you have the pass.

14. Troubleshooting

No tournament

There is no event this week.

No players this week

No players are registered. Add someone from the Leaderboard, or enter an ID in Connect IQ.

Field not announced yet

The list of players for the event has not been published yet.

No data, with a number

The data did not arrive. Check that your phone is online and paired with the watch, then open the screen again.

A dot does not move

It may be on the green: see Following a player around the course in chapter 4. Dots are also about 1–2 minutes behind.

Trial expired or License required

A pass screen was opened without an active pass. Buy the 1-Year Pass: see chapter 1. Your players and settings stay on the watch.

How long the pass lasts

365 days from the day you buy. There is no auto-renewal and nothing to cancel. To continue, buy it again. Your receipt and key are in the purchase email.

The glance shows an old score

The glance shows what the app last loaded. Open the app once.
