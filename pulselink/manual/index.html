PulseLink — User Manual

A complete walkthrough of every screen, menu and setting.

1. Getting Started

Before you can use PulseLink on your watch, you need a free account. Sign up on the website, install the app via Connect IQ, and enter your credentials on your phone — your watch logs in automatically.

  1. Sign up at easymoat.github.io/pulselink/register — you'll receive a welcome email.
  2. Install PulseLink on your watch from the Connect IQ Store.
  3. In Connect IQ Mobile on your phone: open PulseLink settings, enter your email and password.
  4. Open PulseLink on your watch — it logs in automatically and shows the Nickname / Role setup screens.

2. Roles & Trial

Every time PulseLink starts, you choose a role: Host or Guest. Guests are free forever and join with an invite code. Hosts create rooms and need a subscription — but the first 14 days of hosting are free.

Host states explained

  • 14 days free — You haven't hosted yet. The trial starts when you create your first room.
  • N days left — Trial in progress. Subscribe before it ends to keep hosting.
  • your own room — Subscribed. You can create and manage rooms freely.
  • Trial expired — Trial ended without subscribing. Host is locked; Guest still works.

3. Main Screen

The main screen shows every member of your room with their live heart rate, steps and distance. Tap or long-press different rows for different actions.

Interactions

  • Tap anywhere on screen / START button (short press): refresh — push your latest data and pull other members. This is the only send path when Update interval is set to Manual.
  • Long-press the room name row (top): open the PulseLink menu (Sharing, Update interval, Share, Display, Room admin, Leave).
  • UP button (long press): open the Messages feed from anywhere on the main screen.
  • Long-press a member row: open the member menu — Send message (template message to that member; recipient can be switched to Everyone or the host on the send screen) or Move to... (change that member's position in your list).
  • Long-press your own row: open the You menu (Move to..., Heat stress, Alerts, Change password).
  • Long-press the date row (bottom): open the Messages feed (same as UP long-press).
  • Long-press weather bar — left half: open the info loop (weather details → compass → altitude → pressure).
  • Long-press weather bar — right half: jump directly to the compass page (same loop, page 3 — DOWN/UP to cycle).

Color coding

  • Yellow nickname (with Z icon): host is paused or away.
  • Red date: unread messages in the feed — open Messages to clear.
  • HR number color: white by default. With the Heat stress monitor ON, the HR number is colored by that member's heat tier, from Safe (blue) up to Danger (red) — see chapter 8 for the full color scale and how tiers are shared.

4. Sharing & Privacy

PulseLink gives you three independent layers of control over what your watch sends to the room. None of these affect what others share — only your own broadcast.

  • Sharing (master toggle) — OFF stops all transmission instantly. Other members see you as paused.
  • Share → Heart rate / Weather — Toggle individual data types. HR off means others see your steps in the HR position.
  • Update interval — How often you broadcast. Host sets it for the whole room (10s ~ 10min, or Manual). Guest can follow host or override locally to Manual.
Battery tip: Longer Update interval = less data, less battery. 5 min is fine for casual runs; 10 sec for race day.

5. Display

The Display menu controls what appears on your screen — your own weather bar, other members' weather, background color and clock color.

  • My weather — Show / hide the bottom weather bar (your own conditions).
  • Member weather — Show / hide other members' weather in their rows.
  • My wx color / Background / Clock color — Personalize the look — 6 backgrounds, 12 clock colors, 6 weather bar colors.

6. Messages

PulseLink uses template messages — short fixed phrases you compose ahead of time. You get 20 slots, with 5 pre-filled defaults. Edit them in the Connect IQ Mobile app.

How to send

  1. Long-press a member row on the main screen.
  2. Pick a template from the list.
  3. Recipient defaults to that member. Tap the To: row to switch to Everyone or the host.

Reading messages

Unread count shows on the room name row (N new in red). To open the feed, either long-press the UP button or long-press the date row at the bottom of the main screen — both jump straight to the messages.

7. Room Admin (Host)

Hosts have a Room admin submenu with full control over the room — who can join, who gets banned, the room name and invite code.

  • Join requests — Approve or reject pending guests. Number appears in the submenu badge.
  • Members — Tap any member to kick (temporary) or ban (permanent).
  • Banned users — Review and unban.
  • Host mode — Participate (broadcast your data) or Observe (hidden, no broadcast).
  • Rename room / Invite code reset — Tap to update. Invite code resets immediately — the old code stops working.

8. Heat Stress Guide

A personal heat-strain indicator that combines current temperature, humidity, your heart rate and Body Battery into a single 5-level rating. Information only — not medical advice.

Opening & toggling

  1. Long-press your own row on the main screen → You menu → Heat stress.
  2. Tap the Monitor button at the top of the screen, or press START, to toggle ON / OFF. Default is OFF (seasonal opt-in).

What you see

  • A 5-level tier with color: Safe = blue, Caution = green, Warning = yellow, Severe = orange, Danger = red.
  • Estimated WBGT, Feels like, Humidity.
  • Your current HR and %HRR (with a note if your HR is high for the current heat).
  • A short advisory line that scrolls if long.

On the main screen

When Monitor is ON, your HR number on the main screen is colored by the current heat tier (blue → red, same scale as above). Members who also have Monitor ON share their tier with the room — you see their HR in their own color too. Members with Monitor OFF stay white. The assessment runs every 60 seconds.

9. Compass

A simple rotating compass for trail runners and hikers, behind the weather bar.

How to open

Long-press the right half of your weather bar on the main screen.

Known behavior — compass may freeze: Due to Garmin's CPU budget for widget-type apps, if the compass updates rapidly for an extended period (e.g. while moving your arm continuously), the system may suspend its drawing thread. The screen will freeze on the last frame. This is normal Garmin power management behavior, not a crash.

To recover: Press BACK to leave the compass, then restart PulseLink (close and reopen). The compass works again.

10. Weather

The bottom weather bar shows current conditions synced from your phone — temperature, humidity, wind direction & speed, pressure, altitude, and an icon for the weather state.

Wind direction icon

A kite shape (chevron) — the pointed end shows where the wind is heading, the notched end shows where it's coming from. This is the standard meteorological convention.

11. Account

Most account tasks are done on the website, not the watch.

  • Register: easymoat.github.io/pulselink/register
  • Forgot password: easymoat.github.io/pulselink/forgot
  • Delete account: easymoat.github.io/pulselink/delete
  • Change password (on watch): Long-press your own row → You menu → Change password.
  • Cancel subscription: Use the Stripe Customer Portal link in your activation email.

12. Troubleshooting

Compass froze on the last frame

See chapter 9 — this is Garmin's CPU budget protection. Press BACK and restart PulseLink.

Stuck on "Connecting..."

  • Check that your watch can reach your phone (Bluetooth connected, Connect IQ Mobile running).
  • Confirm your email and password in Connect IQ Mobile → PulseLink settings — even one wrong character blocks login.

"No weather data"

Your phone hasn't synced weather to the watch recently. Open the Garmin Connect app on your phone with internet access, then re-open PulseLink.

Trial expired but I want to keep hosting

Find your Device ID on the Role select screen (under Host), then subscribe at easymoat.github.io/pulselink — you'll receive an activation key by email.
