Custom Compass Image

Put your own emblem at the center of the PulseLink compass — a family crest, team logo, or any symbol you love. Shown on watches with an active host license.

What makes an ideal image

  • Black background is best. The compass screen is black, so a black-background image blends in seamlessly — no visible edges. Transparent PNG backgrounds also work (treated as black).
  • Square, 400×400 px or larger. It will be scaled down to about 240–300 px on the watch. Non-square images are center-cropped.
  • The whole image is visible. On the watch, your image sits inside the compass ring (about 66% of the screen), so nothing overlaps it — feel free to use the full circle for your design.
  • Bright, high-contrast colors. White, yellow, cyan and red pop on the black screen. Dark colors sink into the background.
  • Simple symbols beat photos. The watch screen is small — fine detail gets lost. To fit the watch's memory the image is also reduced to a limited color palette, so subtle shades and smooth gradients (as in photos) are approximated and won't reproduce exactly. Logos, crests and bold shapes with flat, distinct colors look best.
