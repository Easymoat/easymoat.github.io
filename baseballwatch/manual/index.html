BaseballWatch — User Manual

A complete walkthrough of every screen, gesture and setting.

1. Getting Started

BaseballWatch brings live pro baseball to your wrist. Install it, register your favorite players (or pick a favorite team), and the watch keeps you in the game — live score, count, runners, and real-time event notifications. Every feature is free for the first 14 days.

  1. Install BaseballWatch on your watch from the Connect IQ Store.
  2. Open the app — the 14-day full-feature trial starts automatically. No payment, no account.
  3. To keep using it after the trial: subscribe on the BaseballWatch homepage ($1.49/month via Stripe, cancel anytime). You'll receive an activation key by email.
  4. Enter the key in Connect IQ Mobile → BaseballWatch → Settings → License Key, then restart the app.
Game data comes from the public baseball stats API and reaches your watch through your phone — keep the watch paired with Garmin Connect Mobile and the phone online.

How often the screen updates

BaseballWatch adapts its update rate to the game situation, so it's fastest when it matters and battery-friendly the rest of the time:

SituationUpdate interval
Your player is at bat / on the moundabout 5 seconds
Game is live (player waiting)about 15 seconds
Before first pitchramps up as the start approaches: 5 min (2 h before) → 30 s (20 min before) → 15 s (5 min before)
No game today / far from startabout 1 hour

2. Two Modes

BaseballWatch has two display modes. Team Mode is the easiest way to follow a whole team; Player Mode tracks specific players across teams. Switch anytime: tap the upper part of the main screen to open Settings, then choose Display Mode.

ModeWhat it shows
Team ModePick one favorite team. The 20 slots fill automatically with today's lineup and pitchers, and the screen auto-focuses the current batter or pitcher live (chapter 8).
Player ModeUp to 20 players of your choice, from any teams. UP/DOWN cycles through them; each screen is built around one player and their game today.
Your registered players and your favorite team are stored separately — switching modes never erases the other mode's setup.

3. Managing Players (Player Mode only)

Player Mode tracks players by their official Player ID. Find any player's ID with the lookup tool on the BaseballWatch homepage — search by name and copy the number.

On the watch (Member List)

  1. Open the settings menu (tap the upper area) and choose Member List. The 20 slots appear, each showing the player's name and a status marker — #slot number, plus W (watched) or L (lock-in), or OFF when the slot is disabled.
  2. Select a slot to open its action menu. A filled slot offers Select Player (show it on the main screen), Reorder, Edit ID, Enabled (on/off) and Add Player; an empty slot offers just Edit ID and Add Player.
  3. Edit ID opens a number pad — type the Player ID and confirm. Entering 0 empties the slot.

Watched & locked-in players

In Player Mode each player has three states. Long-press the upper part of the main screen to cycle the displayed player through them (white → green → pink → green → white):

  • Off (white) — not watched; no notifications and no auto-switch.
  • Watched (green) — notifications fire and the screen auto-switches to this player on their events. This is the W mark in the Member List.
  • Lock-in (pink) — the screen pins to this player. While locked in, other watched players never pull the screen away and their notifications are held — perfect when two of your players are in action at once. Switching off lock-in moves to the next active watched player automatically.

Both Watched (green) and Lock-in (pink) can also be set from the Member List. In Team Mode every player of your team is watched automatically.

5. Notifications & Effects

Real-time notifications

When a watched player steps to the plate or takes the mound, and when they produce a result — hit, home run, walk, strikeout and more — the watch vibrates, plays a tone, and switches the screen to that player. Home runs ring stronger than hits; appearances ring lighter.

How notifications behave is set in Settings → Notification:

OptionBehavior
Sound + Vibe + SwitchTone, vibration, and auto-switch to the player (default)
Sound + SwitchTone and auto-switch, no vibration
Vibe + SwitchVibration and auto-switch, no tone
Switch OnlySilent — the screen just switches

Event effects (animations)

Big moments get a celebration on top of the notification:

  • Single — bronze ball, with confetti (about 5 seconds)
  • Double — silver ball, with confetti
  • Triple — gold ball, with confetti
  • Home run — diamond (gem) ball, with fireworks and confetti
  • Strikeout — white ball with a red K, swing-and-miss slashes, plus a snappy vibration (about 2.4 seconds). Fires when your pitcher records a strikeout.
  • Stolen base — speed lines and a dust burst (about 3 seconds), when a watched runner steals.
  • Your team's win — a waving flag with fireworks (Team Mode), a short while after your team's game ends in a win.

Statcast on the play

Hit and home-run animations overlay the batted-ball data — the exit velocity (mph), and for home runs the distance (ft) too. And on the live main screen, the last pitch's speed and type (e.g. 96 FOUR) show by home plate inside the diamond, so you can read the radar gun every pitch. When the stadium doesn't supply this data, the figures simply don't appear.

6. Browse Mode

Peek at any player in the current game — step through the batting or pitching order, or check a runner — without losing the live view. The score, count and runners keep updating while you browse. There are two ways to drive it, by touch or by buttons; the feature is the same.

By touch

ActionWhat it does
Long-press batter name (right)Enter batting-order browse, starting from the current batter
Long-press pitcher name (left)Enter pitching-order browse (starter → current pitcher)
UP / DOWN / SwipeStep to the previous / next player in the order
Tap a baseShow the runner on that base instantly; tap the same base again to return
BACK / long-press againExit browse and return to the live player

By buttons — the selection frame

Long-press UP and a selection frame appears on the field — handy mid-run or whenever touch is awkward. Move the frame to any on-field player (batter, pitcher, runners) and confirm.

ActionWhat it does
Long-press UPShow the selection frame (starts on the batter)
UP / DOWN / SwipeMove the frame: batter → pitcher → runners on base (cyclic). When the frame lands on a runner, that runner is displayed immediately.
STARTConfirm — for a batter or pitcher, browse that order (UP / DOWN to the previous / next player)
BACKFrom a selected view: back to the frame. From the frame: exit to the normal screen.
While the frame is shown, START is the picker (it does not open the inning history). Close the frame with BACK first, then START opens the history (chapter 7).

The browsed player's day — at-bat results so far, live stats — appears in the main area, so you can review the whole lineup between pitches.

7. Inning History

Press START on the main screen to replay the whole game, half-inning by half-inning — both teams included. In Team Mode it shows your team's game; in Player Mode, the displayed player's game.

Reading the screen

  • Header: the batting team's abbreviation + the half-inning — TOP n in cyan, BOT n in red, so you always know which side is up.
  • Each row: batting-order number, batter's name, and the result code. +1 / +2 after the code is the RBI count.
  • Base-running rows: some rows show a runner's move instead of an at-bat — the runner's name with a code such as SB2 (stole 2nd). Steals, pickoffs and wild-pitch advances appear before the batter, tag-ups and outs on the bases after — all in event order.
  • Bottom: P: the pitcher who threw that half-inning. A mid-inning relief shows both, e.g. Dreyer>Klein. Below it, 3 OUTS (in red) marks a completed inning, or - in progress - for the current one.

Result codes

CodeMeaning
1B / 2B / 3B / HRSingle / double / triple / home run. A suffix shows the direction: -L left, -C center, -R right (HR-C = homer to center). A trailing -O means the batter was thrown out trying for an extra base (2B-C-O = doubled to center, out advancing).
GO / FLY / LINE / POPGround out / fly out / line out / pop out, with the fielder: GO-SS = grounder to short, FLY-L = fly to left.
K / K-DPStrikeout / strikeout double play (shown in red)
BB / IBB / HBPWalk / intentional walk / hit by pitch (light blue)
GIDP / TPGrounded into double play / triple play (orange)
SF / SAC / FC / E / FORCESacrifice fly / sacrifice bunt / fielder's choice / reached on error / force out
SB / CS / POBase-running by a runner (not the batter): stolen base / caught stealing / picked off, with the base — SB2, CS2, PO1.
WP / PB / TAG / OA / FERunner advanced on a wild pitch (WP) / passed ball (PB) / tag-up (TAG), thrown out advancing (OA), or advanced on a fielding error (FE) — with the base reached: WP3, TAGH (home), OA3, FE2.

Hits glow green, home runs gold — scan an inning at a glance and spot where the damage happened.

Navigation

ActionWhat it does

The history opens on the current (most recent) half-inning, so a quick START press answers "what just happened?" — then UP rewinds the game.

8. Team Mode

Follow a whole team instead of individual players. Set your team in Settings → Favorite Team, then switch Display Mode to Team.

  • The 20 slots fill automatically with today's lineup (batting order 1–9) and the pitchers — substitutions included as the game goes on.
  • Live, the screen auto-focuses your team's current batter when batting and your pitcher when fielding.
  • Every player of your team is watched — all notifications and event animations (chapter 5) are active, including the strikeout K for your pitcher.

Division standings

Open the settings menu (tap the upper area) and choose Standings. Each row shows a team's abbreviation, win–loss record, games behind (GB) and current streak; the header names the division.

ActionWhat it does
UP / DOWN / SwipeCycle through all six divisions (AL / NL East, Central, West) — the header shows which one (e.g. 2/6).
Long-press a team rowPeek at that team's game in Team Mode, then return to the main screen — your saved favorite team is untouched.
Tap / START / BACKClose and return to the main screen.

9. Detail Screen

Long-press the center of the diamond to open the displayed player's detail screen — it also works while browsing another player.

  • At Bats — every at-bat result of today's game (AB 1: Walk, AB 2: Flyout, ...)
  • Game Pitching — for pitchers: pitch count, strikes, strike %, decision, walks, HBP and home runs allowed
  • Season — season totals: AVG / HR / RBI / OPS for batters, W-L / ERA / IP / K / WHIP / SV for pitchers

Add / remove from your Player Mode list

At the bottom of the detail screen is a button — Add (green) when the player isn't in your Player Mode list, or Remove (red) when they are. Long-press the button to toggle. Adding also turns that player's watch (notifications) on; removing turns it off. If the list is already full (20 players), a List Full message appears instead. This makes Team Mode handy for discovery — open a teammate's detail and add them to your personal list in one long-press. START or BACK closes the detail screen.

11. Troubleshooting

The screen shows Waiting... / no game

The player's team simply has no game today, or the day's games haven't started. The screen shows the start time once a game is scheduled.

Data stops updating

Check that the watch is connected to Garmin Connect Mobile and the phone has internet. During a live game updates flow about every 15 seconds — and about every 5 seconds while your player is at bat or on the mound (see the update-interval table in chapter 1).

No notifications arrive

Check Settings → Notification isn't set lower than you expect, and — in Player Mode — that the player has the W (watch) flag in the Member List. In Team Mode all players notify automatically.

A registered player never shows data

The Player ID is probably wrong. Look the player up by name with the homepage lookup tool and re-enter the exact numeric ID (Member List → Edit ID).

Inning history says No game today

The team being looked up (your favorite team, or the displayed player's team) has no game today. During off days the history has nothing to show.

An animation didn't play

Effects play for watched batters' hits and homers, and for your pitcher's strikeouts, during live games. While you're browsing or the selection frame is open, effects hold back so they don't interrupt you — the vibration and tone still fire.

Trial expired

Subscribe on the homepage ($1.49/month), enter the emailed key in Connect IQ settings → License Key, and restart the app. Your players and settings are still on the watch and come back after activation.

Managing / cancelling the subscription

Use the Stripe Customer Portal — the link is in your activation email and every Stripe receipt. Cancelling keeps full access until the end of the paid period.
