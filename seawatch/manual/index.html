SeaWatch — User Manual

Every screen, gesture and setting.

1. Getting Started

SeaWatch puts the coast on your Garmin. Zoom from a map of the whole region down to an aerial photo of one spot, then open its tides, swell, wind, water temperature, alerts and weather. It is made for surfing and fishing.

  1. Install SeaWatch on your watch from the Connect IQ Store.
  2. Open the app. A map of the USA comes first.
  3. Tap a blue dot to choose a region, then tap it again and press OK. The maps for that region download to your watch.
  4. When the download ends, the region map opens. Tap the coast to zoom in, and keep going until you reach a spot.
  5. Tap OPEN> at the bottom to see the spot's tides and conditions.
Maps, tides and conditions reach your watch through your phone. Keep the watch paired with Garmin Connect Mobile and the phone online.

2. Choosing a Region

Your watch holds one region at a time. Choosing another region replaces the one on the watch.

Do thisWhat happens
Tap a dot, or UP / DOWN (or swipe)Selects a region. Its name appears at the bottom.
Tap the selected dot again, tap the bottom row, or STARTOpens the region, or asks before downloading it.
BACKCloses the confirmation, or closes the app.

The bottom row

ShowsMeans
OPEN>This region is on your watch. Opens its map.
GET>Not on your watch yet.
CONTINUE 7/19>The download stopped part way. It picks up where it left off.
COMING SOONThis region is not ready yet.

Downloading

The confirmation shows how many maps the region has and about how long it takes, usually about a minute. If another region is on your watch, it also shows REPLACES and that region's name. Press OK to start.

  • BACK during the download pauses it. Next time, choose the region again and it continues.
  • If the phone loses its connection, the watch tries again by itself. If it still cannot connect, NO CONNECTION and RETRY appear.
  • The region on your watch stays until the new one starts arriving, so a failed download does not leave you with nothing.

Updates

When the maps or spots for your region are updated, UPDATE AVAILABLE appears and the watch downloads the new version.

4. What Each Level Shows

Region map

The colors on the sea show the chance of toxic red tide, from purple (0%) to red (100%). The TOXIN bar at the bottom is the key. Clouds come from the latest satellite image.

  • Tap the left button for red tide and the right button for clouds. Each turns on and off, and the watch remembers your choice.
  • The row above the buttons shows the time at the coast and your watch's time (YOU).
  • START jumps to the spot nearest you when My location is on.
  • Where there is no red tide data (Hawaii, for example), only the clouds button appears.

Coast map (20 km)

Spots appear from this level on. The red tide colors run along the shore, and the same two buttons turn red tide and clouds on and off.

Close-up map (5 km)

  • Yellow arrows show the wind over the sea: pale yellow under 5 m/s (about 11 mph), yellow from 5 to 10 m/s, orange 10 m/s (about 22 mph) and up.
  • White arrows show the tidal current at nearby stations.
  • The bottom shows the spot's name, its weather, temperature and wind, then its type and OPEN>.

Aerial map (1 km)

The weather card next to the spot shows, from the top: weather and temperature, chance of rain, humidity, wind and pressure (hPa). Tap the card to open the weather screen (chapter 7).

Bottom rowExampleMeans

Spot photo

A close aerial photo centered on the spot. UP / DOWN shows the next spot's photo. Tap OPEN> or press START to open the spot screen.

6. Tides for 14 Days

Do thisWhat happens
UP / DOWN (or swipe), or tap a barSelects a day. Its tide phase and tides appear below.
Tap the selected bar again, or STARTOpens that day: the curve, highs and lows in time order, and sunrise and sunset.
BACKGoes back to the 14 days, then to the spot screen.

Dates follow the spot's local day. Times follow the LOCAL / MY TIME choice on the spot screen.

7. Weather

Open it by tapping the weather card on the aerial map. Tap or press DOWN for the next page, UP for the previous one, and BACK to return to the map.

PageShows
NowWeather and temperature, feels-like, humidity and pressure, then chance of rain, today's high and low, and wind
Hourly (2 pages)Every 3 hours: time, weather, temperature, wind and chance of rain
Daily (2 pages)Six days from today: day, weather, high, low and chance of rain

The line at the bottom of the first page shows when the weather data is from. It turns yellow when it is more than 3 hours old. Temperatures are in °C. Wind speed follows the Wind unit setting.

8. Glance

The glance shows the last spot you opened and its next high or low tide, with the time and height.

9. Settings

Hold the information area at the bottom of any screen: the maps, the USA map, the spot screen, the tides or the weather. Holding UP (MENU) also works.

ItemWhat it does
Map alerts & infoChooses what the maps show. See below.
My locationON shows where you are on the map and your distance to the spot. Your location stays on the watch and is never sent.
Wind unitAuto, m/s, km/h, mph or knots. Auto uses mph when your watch's distance unit is miles, and m/s otherwise.
Device IDYour watch's ID for SeaWatch. Have it ready if you contact support.
BuildThe version of the app on your watch.

You can also set the wind unit on your phone: Connect IQ → SeaWatch → Settings → Wind speed unit.

10. Troubleshooting

You seeWhat to do
LOADING... staysCheck that the phone is near the watch and online, and that Garmin Connect Mobile is running.
NO CONNECTION or NO REPLYThe phone could not reach SeaWatch. The watch tries again by itself. Reopening the app also helps.
CONTINUE on the USA mapThe region download stopped part way. Select the region and continue.
ALERTS: OLD DATAThe alerts could not be refreshed. Do not rely on them until they update.
ALGAE: NO DATAThere is no harmful algae data for this spot.
NO TIDE STATIONThere is no tide station near this spot.
UPDATED in yellowThe spot data is more than 6 hours old, usually because the phone was offline. Open the spot again while online.
SeaWatch is a guide, not a safety device. Always check official forecasts, warnings and local conditions before you go in the water or out on a boat.
