The list

Open the app and see your people

No rooms to join and nothing to leave. PulseLink opens on the people you are connected with: their heart rate, steps, distance and weather, updating live. Tap a name to see them on the map.

❤️

Live heart rate

See each person's current heart rate, turning orange and red as it climbs.

👟

Steps and distance

Today's steps and distance for everyone, at a glance.

🌤️

Their weather

Temperature and weather where each person is.

🗺️

Map

Tap a person to see them on a line map with roads, rivers and terrain, from 200 m to 100 km. Tap a group to see everyone.

🧭

Radar

A GPS radar on a compass shows your people and saved places: bearing, distance and how long ago each position came in. Navigate to points a big arrow at a place or a person.

💬

Messages

Send a cheer with one tap from 20 templates you write yourself, or type your own. An incoming message appears as a banner, and one tap replies, so you never stop running.

📍

Arrive and leave alerts

Your wrist buzzes when someone reaches or leaves a saved place: a child at school, for example.

📏

Distance alerts

Know when someone drifts away, each time they pass a set distance from 100 m to 50 km, and when they come back.

⌚

Watch face and glance

Put the distance to the top person in your list in a data field of your watch's own face. It keeps updating about every 5 minutes, even with PulseLink closed. The glance shows the same person's name, distance, heart rate and steps.

Privacy

You decide once, together

Sharing starts only when both people agree, and there are no switches to manage. The one who invites picks a level; the other reads one line and accepts or declines.

🚶

Activity

Steps, distance and weather.

💓

+ Heart

Activity, plus heart rate.

📍

+ Location

+ Heart, plus your position on the radar and the map. Your watch sends your position only when you have someone at this level.

⬇️

Lower it any time

Either person can lower a level at once, with no approval. Raising it needs the other person's yes. Ending a connection takes one person.

👨‍👩‍👧

Groups

Up to 20 people in a group, up to 10 groups. Everyone in a group sees each other at its level and talks together. No leader: anyone can lower the level, and only you can take yourself out.

🔕

Manual interval

Choose how often your watch sends, from 10 seconds to 10 minutes, or set Manual to send only when you press START.

Your screens

A clock, a picture and your whole day

Beyond the people, PulseLink has screens of your own. Each can show a clock, and the look is set once for all of them.

🖼️

Your picture

Send up to 5 pictures from the web and pick one on your watch. Turn the picture off for a clean black background.

📊

16 values on one screen

Weather, pressure, wind, chance of rain, altitude, heart rate, steps, floors, calories, weight, Body Battery, stress, respiration, the moon, and the next sunrise or sunset.

🕰️

Clock styles

Analog Hands, minimalist Rim marks, or a large Digital readout, in the color you choose, with an optional halo so it reads over any picture.

📷

A camera on your wrist

Turn a spare phone into a still camera: a pet at home, the garden, the front door. Change the zoom and angle from your watch, get a buzz when something moves, and watch it big on any screen with the Viewer.

🔋

Battery Saver

After a few seconds with no touch, the screen dims to just the clock and date.

Subscribe & Activate

The full version in 4 steps

The 14-day free trial starts the first time you open the app. Updating from an earlier version without the full version? Your 14 days start the first time you open the new version. Buy before it ends and everything keeps going.

1

Find your Device ID

Open PulseLink on your watch, press START on the list and open Device ID. It shows an 8-character code like AB12-CD34, with your trial days or licence state.

2

Subscribe

Tap Subscribe above, pay $9.99/year via Stripe, and enter your Garmin Device ID at checkout. An activation key is emailed to you instantly.

3

Enter the key

Garmin Connect IQ app → PulseLink → Settings → paste the key into the "License Key" field.

4

Restart

Save settings and restart PulseLink on your watch. The full version stays unlocked as long as your subscription is active.

Cancel anytime via the Stripe Customer Portal (link in your activation email and Stripe receipt). Access continues until the end of the current billing period.

Subscription & Cancellation

Cancel anytime

Cancellation Policy: The PulseLink full version is an annual subscription that renews automatically. You can cancel anytime via the Stripe Customer Portal — link is included in your activation email and Stripe receipt. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

After cancellation or trial expiry, your watch returns straight away to just you: free, with no time limit. You keep the list, the Image screen and one saved place. Your connections and groups are removed, on the server as well, and purchasing later does not bring them back; you start again by inviting people.

Refund Policy: As PulseLink is a digital service activated by a license key, refunds are not provided once the activation email has been sent. If you experience activation issues, please contact and we'll resolve them promptly.

Privacy

Privacy & Health Data

PulseLink processes heart rate data, which is treated as a special category of personal data under GDPR. We collect only what is necessary: your watch's Device ID, a nickname, and the metrics you agree to show the people you connect with.

You can switch to Manual interval (no auto-send) at any time, lower a level, or end a connection. You can erase everything stored for your watch at any time from the Delete my data page.
