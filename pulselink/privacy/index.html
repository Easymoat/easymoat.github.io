Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

PulseLink is a Garmin Connect IQ app operated by Easymoat (sole proprietor: Yoshicchi). This Privacy Policy explains what data PulseLink processes, why, and the choices you have. By using PulseLink you agree to this policy.

Health data notice. PulseLink processes heart-rate data, which is a special category of personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We process it only with your explicit consent and only to provide the sharing features you choose to use.

1. Who we are

Easymoat (sole proprietor: Yoshicchi), Japan. Contact: easymoat@gmail.com. We are the data controller for the information described below.

2. What we collect

  • Identity data — your watch's Device ID (an identifier the watch already carries) and a nickname you choose. This is the whole of what identifies you to us: we hold no email address, no password, and no real name. To confirm that a reinstalled app belongs to the same watch, we also keep a one-way fingerprint derived from the watch's own device identifier. The identifier itself is not stored and cannot be recovered from the fingerprint. We also keep the date (not the time) of your watch's most recent connection, only to operate the service; it is deleted together with your other data.
  • Activity & health data — heart rate, step count, and distance, read from your Garmin device. These are shared in real time with the people you are connected with, within the level of each connection (see Section 4).
  • Location data (optional) — only within a connection or group at the "+ Location" level: your current position is read from your Garmin device and shown to those people on the radar and the map. It is used for that purpose only. Your watch sends your position only while at least one such connection exists. Lowering the level or ending the connection stops it.
  • Connection data — who you are connected with, the level of each connection, the groups you create and their members, pending invitations, and the template messages you send. If you share your phone camera, we also keep whether camera sharing is on, which connected people you share it with, and whose camera you have chosen to view.
  • Payment data — handled entirely by Stripe. We never see, receive, or store your card number. We only receive a subscription status (active / expired) from Stripe.
  • Map area (map home only) — to draw the map, the watch sends its approximate position rounded to the map's grid — about 25 m at the closest range, coarser at wider ranges. We keep the map tiles we build, but not which watch asked for which area.
  • Trail map (optional) — when you plan a hike, the watch sends the positions of the peaks you pick (2 to 20 points) so we can work out the route and build its map images. We keep the routes and images we build, keyed to the route itself, not to your watch. To prevent abuse we count requests per watch for a short time, in server memory only. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

We do not collect your name, address, contacts, or any advertising identifiers, and we do not build a location history or movement track. PulseLink contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Your position is sent only within a connection at the + Location level, and only your latest position — never a stored trail of where you have been.

On-device profile reading (not collected). To draw certain on-watch displays — the heat-stress advisory and the body-metrics bar — PulseLink reads your weight, age and resting heart rate from your Garmin user profile. This happens only on your watch to render those displays; this profile data is never transmitted to our server, stored, or shared with anyone.

3. How we use your data

  • To tell one watch from another, so your data goes to you and not to someone else (Device ID).
  • To show your heart rate, steps, distance and other data to the people you are connected with, within the level of each connection.
  • At the + Location level, to show your current position to those people on the radar and the map.
  • To deliver template messages between the people you are connected with.
  • To draw the map on your watch face, if you choose that home screen (using the rounded map area above).
  • To work out the hiking route you plan and draw the trail map on your watch, if you use Trail map.
  • To manage full-version purchases via Stripe.

Legal bases (GDPR): your explicit consent for heart-rate (health) data and for sharing your position; performance of a contract for account and connection features; and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

4. Who can see your data

  • People you are connected with. A connection is made when one person invites and the other accepts, choosing a level: Activity (nickname, online status, steps, distance, weather), + Heart (adds heart rate), or + Location (adds your current position). Both people have the same level. Either can lower it at any time without the other's approval; raising it needs the other's acceptance. The server decides what each person receives, so nobody receives more than the level allows.
  • Groups. A group has a level chosen by the person who creates it. Joining a group connects you with every member of that group at that level (you see the invitation's one line and accept or decline). Any member can lower a group's level at any time; it then applies to everyone at once. A group's level cannot be raised — create a new group instead. Leaving a group ends those connections.
  • Images and camera. Images you upload are shown only on your own watch and are never sent to other people. Your phone camera is private too, unless you choose to share it: you can turn camera sharing on and pick, one by one, which connected people may view it, at any level. A person you share with can view your camera picture on their watch (and on a linked The Face dial) only while camera sharing is on, you have selected that person, and the connection is active. Nobody else can view it. Choosing a person is your consent to share the camera with that person; the people you share with do not see your images, and you do not see theirs.
  • The Face (Face Composer), if you link it. When you link a Face Composer dial to this watch with your Web code, that dial receives your approximate position as the place for its weather and the picture from PulseLink Cam. Sign out web on the watch removes the link.
  • Stripe — our payment processor, for full-version purchases only.
  • Garmin — your data travels over the Garmin Connect IQ platform between your watch and your phone.

We never sell your data, and we never share it with advertisers or data brokers.

5. How long we keep it

This reflects how PulseLink is actually built:

  • Heart rate, steps, distance — held only in server memory as the latest value, not written to permanent storage. It is overwritten by your next update (typically within seconds to minutes) and lost entirely if the server restarts.
  • Location — treated the same way: held only in server memory as your latest position, overwritten by your next update and lost if the server restarts. Lowering your last + Location connection, or ending it, clears your stored position immediately.
  • Messages — deleted automatically after 24 hours (at most 200 per conversation).
  • Invitations — expire after 24 hours if not accepted.
  • Camera sharing settings — the on/off switch, the people you share with, and the camera you chose to view are kept until you change them, end the connection, or erase your data. Camera pictures themselves are stored as before (see the camera page); sharing adds no new copies.
  • Connections and groups — kept while your trial or full version is active. When the trial ends without purchase, or your subscription is cancelled or expires, one connection (the person at the top of your list) is kept; all other connections, all groups and pending invitations are excluded immediately and physically removed by an hourly cleanup; those people no longer see you. The remaining connection keeps working at its level. Your identity data remains until you erase it.
  • Identity data (Device ID, nickname, device fingerprint) — kept until you erase it on the Delete my data page. The fingerprint and any web sign-in are erased together with your data.
  • On data deletion — your record, your uploaded images, your connections, your groups, and your messages are permanently and immediately erased.
  • Backups — we keep backups of server data for disaster recovery, encrypted in transit. Data you erase, or that is removed when a trial or paid period ends, disappears from backups within 90 days (images within 60 days). Backups are used only to restore the service after a failure.
  • Service logs — our hosting provider keeps technical logs for 7 days. They may contain your Device ID and the time of a request. They never contain messages, positions or heart rate.

6. Your privacy controls

PulseLink gives you these in-app controls:

  • Stop sharing your camera — turn camera sharing off, or deselect a person; they stop seeing your camera immediately. Ending the connection has the same effect.
  • Lower a level — open the connection and lower the level at any time; it applies to both of you at once and needs no approval. To stop sharing your position with someone, lower that connection below + Location.
  • End a connection — removes you from that person's view immediately.
  • Leave a group — ends the connections made through that group.
  • Manual interval — disables automatic sending; data is sent only when you choose.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location (including under the GDPR for EU/UK users), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Delete all data associated with your watch — at any time, yourself, via the Delete my data page.
  • Rectify your data — change your nickname in the Connect IQ phone app.
  • Withdraw consent for health-data or position processing — lower the level or end the connection using the in-app controls above, or delete your data.
  • Data portability and object to processing — contact us at easymoat@gmail.com.

EU/UK users also have the right to lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.

8. Eligibility

PulseLink requires a Garmin device and a Garmin account. You must meet the eligibility and age requirements of your Garmin account to use PulseLink.

9. Security

All communication between the app and our server is encrypted in transit using HTTPS (TLS). A watch identifies itself with its Device ID, which is displayed only on the watch itself and is therefore known to its owner alone. Heart-rate, step and distance values are held only transiently in server memory (see Section 5). No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we apply measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data.

PulseLink's web pages (image upload, PL Cam, Viewer and data deletion) require a 6-digit Web code shown on your watch, so that knowing a Device ID alone does not give access to your data.

Watchface images (an optional feature) are uploaded from the website over HTTPS and delivered only to your own watch. On the watch the image is rendered solely as a static bitmap through Garmin's image API — shown as pixels only, it cannot run code or access any other data on your device.

10. International transfers

Our server is hosted on Render. Depending on region, your data may be processed on servers located outside your country. We rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

11. Children

PulseLink is not directed at children below the age permitted by your Garmin account terms. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone who does not meet those requirements.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact

Questions or requests: easymoat@gmail.com.

5. 保持期間

これは PulseLink の実際の設計を反映したものです:

  • 心拍数・歩数・距離 — 最新値としてサーバーのメモリ上にのみ保持し、永続ストレージには書き込みません。次回の更新（通常は数秒〜数分以内）で上書きされ、サーバー再起動時には完全に失われます。
  • 位置情報 — 上記と同様に、最新の現在地としてサーバーのメモリ上にのみ保持し、次回の更新で上書き、サーバー再起動時に失われます。最後の + Location のつながりのレベルを下げるか解消すると、保存済みの位置は即座に削除されます。
  • メッセージ — 24 時間後に自動で削除されます（1 つの会話に最大 200 件）。
  • 招待 — 承認されないまま 24 時間たつと失効します。
  • カメラの共有の設定 — 共有の ON/OFF、共有している相手、見るカメラとして選んだ相手は、変更するか、つながりを解消するか、データを消去するまで保持します。カメラの画像そのものの保存はこれまでと同じで、共有によって新しい写しは増えません。
  • つながりとグループ — 試用または有料版が有効な間だけ保持します。購入なく試用が終わった時、またはサブスクリプションを解約・満了した時は、つながり 1 名（一覧のいちばん上の人）だけが残り、ほかのつながり・すべてのグループ・保留中の招待はすぐに対象から外れ、1 時間ごとの整理で物理的に削除されます。その人たちからあなたは見えなくなります。残った 1 名とのつながりは、そのレベルのまま続きます。本人識別情報は、消去するまで残ります。
  • 本人識別情報（Device ID・ニックネーム・照合値） — データ削除ページで消されるまで保持します。照合値と Web のサインイン情報も、データと同時に削除されます。
  • データ削除の実行時 — 記録、アップロードした画像、つながり、グループ、メッセージを恒久的かつ即時に消去します。
  • バックアップ — 障害からの復旧のために、サーバーのデータのバックアップを保持します（転送時は暗号化）。消去したデータや、試用・有料期間の終了で削除されたデータは、最長 90 日（画像は 60 日）でバックアップからも消えます。バックアップは障害後の復旧にだけ使います。
  • サービスのログ — ホスティング事業者が技術的なログを 7 日間保持します。ログには Device ID と要求の時刻が含まれることがあります。メッセージ・位置・心拍は含まれません。

6. プライバシー制御

PulseLink はアプリ内に以下の制御を備えています:

  • カメラの共有をやめる — カメラの共有を OFF にするか、相手の選択を外すと、その人はすぐにあなたのカメラを見られなくなります。つながりの解消でも同じです。
  • レベルを下げる — つながりを開いていつでも下げられます。2 人に同時に効き、承認は要りません。位置を見せたくない相手とは、そのつながりを + Location より下に下げます。
  • つながりを解消する — すぐに相手からあなたが見えなくなります。
  • グループから抜ける — そのグループを通じたつながりが解消されます。
  • 手動インターバル（Manual） — 自動送信を無効化し、お客様が選んだ時のみ送信します。

9. セキュリティ

アプリとサーバー間の通信はすべて HTTPS（TLS）で暗号化されています。ウォッチは Device ID で自身を示します。この ID はウォッチの画面にしか表示されないため、所有者だけが知り得ます。心拍数・歩数・距離の値はサーバーのメモリ上に一時的に保持されるのみです（第5項参照）。完全に安全な送信・保存方法は存在しませんが、データの機微性に見合った措置を講じています。

PulseLink の Web ページ（画像アップロード、PL Cam、Viewer、データ削除）を開くには、ウォッチに表示される 6 桁の Web code が必要です。Device ID を知っているだけでは、お客様のデータにアクセスできないようにするためです。

ウォッチフェイス画像（任意の機能）は、ウェブから HTTPS でアップロードされ、あなたのウォッチにだけ配信されます。ウォッチ上では Garmin の画像 API を通じて静止画像（ビットマップ）としてのみ描画され、ピクセルとして表示されるだけで、コードの実行や端末内の他データへのアクセスはできません。

10. 国際的なデータ移転

当社のサーバーは Render 上でホスティングされています。地域によっては、お客様の国外に所在するサーバーでデータが処理される場合があります。当社はそのような移転に適切な保護措置を講じます。
